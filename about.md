Sure Yeah is the electronic music project of Surya Krishnan, a Toronto-based DJ, producer, live performer and event organizer. A self-taught musician on piano, bass guitar and violin, he began producing in 2014, first through hiphop and later through ambient electronic works presented at the Toronto Lights Festival.

His sound as a DJ leans on techno’s diversity and on a lot of music that isn’t techno at all. His sets have a foundation in deep hypnotic techno, industrial techno, dub and bass music, occasionally layering experimental, ambient and drone to pull listeners into more reflective, abstract territory. These result in sets which can be bright and bubbly, [like this one](https://soundcloud.com/non-verbal-communication/pondcast-005), or dark and cavernous, [like this one](https://soundcloud.com/another-setting-radio/071226-sure-yeah-b2b-utopos). He’s a regular presence in the city’s DIY party circuit and recently played Ontario's Best Out of Town music festival.

His productions and live sets are focused on pushing electronics to their physical and creative limits. He draws inspiration from techno’s Midwest roots, the aggression of Birmingham’s industrial sound, and the modern, West Coast sound design-influenced techno coming out of scenes in Italy, Spain and Japan.

He is also one of the organizers behind Second Nature, a Toronto-based events series & collective devoted to the outer reaches of dance music.
